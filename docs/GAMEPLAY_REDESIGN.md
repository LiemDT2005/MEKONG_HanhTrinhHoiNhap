# Gameplay redesign plan

1. Preserve the financial engine and map. Add chapter definitions, atomic strategic decisions, persistent decision records, and outcome-based objectives. Keep each chapter as one existing accounting period; retain `quarter` internally for save compatibility.
2. Put illustrated scenarios, character dialogue, three cost/risk choices, deterministic forecasts, and educational advisor feedback into the world. Require a chapter decision before operating; retain contextual investments and markets.
3. Persist chapter debriefs through the existing history and decisions. Make the final report explain accumulation, technology, labor, and circulation, with a balanced competitiveness win condition.
4. Validate existing and new engine behavior after each stage, build the app, and inspect the playable UI if browser tooling is available.

Compatibility: old financial histories remain untouched. Old saves without story records receive an empty decision log; completed periods are reported as legacy play rather than invented player decisions. Existing calculation and purchase APIs remain usable for comparison scenarios.

Economic boundary: all prices, thresholds, demand, quality scores, timing, and score rules are hypothetical educational assumptions. Accounting profit is not Marxian surplus value; equipment/wages are not a computed organic composition of capital. Agreement descriptions link to official background sources and do not simulate actual tariff eligibility.
